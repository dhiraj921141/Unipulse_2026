# 🎓 Unipulse - Maharashtra College Finder

## 🌟 Project Overview
**Unipulse** is Maharashtra's most comprehensive college finder platform, designed to help students discover their perfect educational institution across all 36 districts of Maharashtra.

## 🚀 Key Achievements

### 📊 Database Expansion
- **From 50 to 1000+ colleges** across all categories
- **Complete coverage** of all 36 Maharashtra districts
- **Engineering**: 500+ colleges with all branches
- **Medical**: 300+ colleges (MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, Nursing)
- **Pharmacy**: 200+ colleges with detailed information
- **Comprehensive data**: Cutoff ranks, marks, fees, ratings, establishment year

### 🤖 AI Integration
- **Google Gemini API** integration for intelligent responses
- **Universal AI Assistant** - answers ANY question (not just college-related)
- **Personalized recommendations** based on marks and preferences

- **Rate limiting** and error handling
