# Maharashtra College Finder

A comprehensive web application to help students find the best engineering, pharmacy, and medical colleges in Maharashtra based on their entrance exam scores (MHT CET for engineering/pharmacy and NEET for medical).

## Features

### 🎯 **Smart College Matching**
- **Engineering Colleges**: Find your best-fit engineering college based on your MHT CET score
- **Pharmacy Colleges**: Find your best-fit pharmacy college based on your MHT CET score  
- **Medical Colleges**: Find your best-fit MBBS college based on your NEET score

### 🔍 **Advanced Filtering System**
- **Category-wise Cutoffs**: General, SC, ST, OBC, EWS
- **College Type**: Government, Private, Autonomous
- **Branch Preferences**: 15+ engineering branches, pharmacy specializations
- **District-wise Search**: All major districts in Maharashtra
- **Rank-based Eligibility**: Enter your rank to see admission chances

### 📊 **Comprehensive Data**
- **500+ Colleges** across Maharashtra
- **Real Cutoff Ranks** for all categories
- **Detailed College Information**: Fees, establishment year, ratings
- **Branch-wise Cutoffs** for precise matching

### 💡 **User-Friendly Features**
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Smart Search**: Search by college name or location
- **Eligibility Indicators**: Visual indicators for admission chances
- **Sort Options**: Sort by cutoff ranks, college names
- **Modern UI**: Clean, intuitive interface with smooth animations

## How to Use

### 1. **Choose Your Field**
Select from three main categories:
- **Engineering** (MHT CET based)
- **Pharmacy** (MHT CET based)
- **Medical** (NEET based)

### 2. **Enter Your Details**
- **Your Rank**: Enter your entrance exam rank
- **Category**: Select your category (General, SC, ST, OBC, EWS)
- **Preferences**: Choose branch, district, college type

### 3. **Get Results**
- View colleges with **eligibility indicators**:
  - 🟢 **High chance** of admission
  - 🟡 **Moderate chance** of admission
  - 🔴 **Low chance** of admission

### 4. **Make Informed Decisions**
- Compare colleges by fees, ratings, location
- View detailed cutoff ranks for all branches
- Sort results by your preferences
